# Source Code

← [[ninety-nine/App Overview|App Overview]]

Engine modules in `ninety-nine/src/`. All conform to Doc 4's technical build spec, except where its dated amendment notes (2026-10-03, 2026-10-16) say the build moved on: the game is one continuous first-person walk, and there are no mini-games or choice buttons.

- **[[ninety-nine/src/main.js|main.js]]**: Doc 4 §2. Boots and runs the preflight, builds the stage and the director, and puts the candidate in his apartment. Two dev-only URL parameters: `?start=S5&render=H` starts the walk at that scene instead (`director.start()`, which also reads `&conf=` / `&diss=`), and `?autopilot=` loads `dev/autopilot.js`. Once the ending card is up it calls `stage.stop()`: nothing is drawn under the opaque card.
- **[[ninety-nine/src/state.js|state.js]]**: Doc 4 §3. The state module (conformance, dissonance, friction, flags). Only `commitChoice()` writes conformance and dissonance; friction comes from `friction.js`, through the director.
- **[[ninety-nine/src/director.js|director.js]]**: replaces the old router. It plays the spine (Doc 1 §4) as one continuous walk through one building ([[ninety-nine/src/world/World|world/]]).
  - **Entering a scene**: advances the drone's detune, crossfades the ambience, sets the music, starts the branch's `hum` if he is in its room, lerps the room to the branch's `env`, schedules the captions and applies the fracture. It runs the scene's `onEnter` beats, starts its driven actors and its `unseen` watches, then arms its two **thresholds**: zones in the room, named by `data/scenes.json`.
  - **Each frame**: inside a threshold's approach ring, its label fades in and its `approach` beats run. Stepping into the zone commits the choice: `commitChoice`, `setFlag`, the movement friction, then the threshold's `beat`s (with the committed zone, so `sit {into}` knows the chair).
  - **Beats** (`runBeats`, the vocabulary `scenes.json`'s `_comment` lists): sounds (`sfxCue` with `sfxOpts` {volume, detuneCents}, `oneShot`, `music`), `caption`, doors (`rattle`, `open`, `close`, `seal`; an `open` with `amount` 0..1 swings the door only that far and leaves it locked), `show` / `hide` (a named prop, or a box with an `id`), `light` {id, on, seconds} (a fade when `seconds` is given), `env` {fog, background, exposure, ambient, sun, grain, vignette, base, seconds} (the room's own atmosphere, through `world.setEnv`), `breath` {every, gain, filterHz, durationMs, inhale} (a breath cue every `every` s; `false` stops it) and `footfall` {gain, filterHz, durationMs} (heavier footsteps through `player.setFootstep`; `false` gives the floors theirs back), both cleared when a scene starts and at the ending, `screen`, `display` {prop, text} (a new reading on a text-capable prop: the cab's floorind), `swing` {prop, amount, decay, axis, period} (a damped swing about the top of a named prop's bounds; knocks add, and swings are stepped in the tick and dropped with their room), `setFlag`, `hold` / `drop` (a prop carried low in frame; held with a `hide` of a prop of the same type, it keeps that prop's material), `actor` {name, from (0..1 or `'here'`), to, speed, stopNear}, and the carried moves `lookAt`, `sit`, `moveTo`, `shot`, `ride`, `shake`. `together` runs a list of carried beats as one move, without the controls snapping him back to standing height between them.
  - **Branch timers**: `idle` {seconds, after, beat, commit} runs when nothing has been chosen for that long (counted only once he has stood in zone `after`), and may commit a threshold for him. `stillness` {seconds, beat} runs when he has stayed within 5 cm of where he stopped for that long (S4 H; turning his head is not moving).
  - **Out of view**: `unseen` {after, point, r, beat} (one or a list) runs once he has stood in zone `after`, on the first frame a sphere round `point` (room-local [x, z]; default the entry doorway) is wholly outside the camera's frustum and he stands more than r + 0.6 m from it. The sphere is max(r, 1) m plus 0.18 m per metre of distance, a margin for the turn he may make next. It is tested after the player moves and before the frame is drawn, so the change is never seen (S4 C's aisle closing up).
  - **Driven actors**: an actor whose record has `drive` {by, lead, ratchet, to, footsteps} is walked by the candidate's progress (`startDrives` at the scene's start, `stepDrive` every frame while the room is the scene's and nothing is being committed). His place on his path is the candidate's room-local `by` coordinate plus `lead` metres (S4 H: 20 m), and with `ratchet` he never comes back. Every `footsteps` m he moves, the branch's `drive.footstep` beats run; at `to` its `drive.end` beats run, and once they have played a zone whose `armAfter` names him arms.
  - **The store's hum**: a branch's or ending's `hum` starts when he is in its room (S8 H: as he lands in the store; EXPULSION keeps it on). It fades once a door it opened has shut behind him (the PENDING route's staff door, as he walks on down `CN_CORRIDOR_SERVICE`) or the room is dropped, and at the card. A step into that passage and back keeps it.
  - **The way on**: if the next scene's room is the same set, the next scene starts where he stands. Otherwise `openWay()` attaches the threshold's `via` connector to its `exit` door and joins the next room behind it, in whichever render the score picks after the choice. A `viaDark` connector stays unlit until he is in it. While the room's door stands in the doorway, the connector's own entry leaf is hidden, and `stage.warm()` compiles what was just placed.
  - **Sealing**: the door behind seals once he is 1.2 m into the connector, and the old room is dropped. Crossing the connector's midpoint is the scene boundary. The next room's entry shuts 1.6 m in, except in a held-entry room (the cab, the store), which holds it open from the moment he is inside until the choice (`seal`).
  - **Carried moments**: `sit` (on the spot, or `into` the committed zone's chair: its `seat` pose, turning round into it), `lookAt` (the call), `moveTo`, `shot`, `rideCab` (the freight cab really descends: one eased ride, its `stops` run as it passes them, the indicator's readings among them as `display` beats; it moves the room from `stage.onPreFrame`, and the room's fixed shaft stays put), `fallInto` (S7 H: the dive joined under the edge's water) and `chuteTo` (S8 H: through the grate into the store). Then the endings: walk into the ending room's zone, or wait out `autoSeconds`; anything he carried is dropped; its `preShot`, `show` (seated, PENDING's slip is already in his hands), its own `beats`, the pull-back, and the hold on a frozen frame (`stage.freeze()`) before the card.
  - **S0**: the APPLY desk, the form laid over the CRT, the portal's answer drawn on the screen, the stairs, the street, the tower's doors and the vestibule. Audio unlocks on the first activating gesture (it listens for `pointerup`, `touchend`, `click` and `keydown` as well as `pointerdown`, since a touch's `pointerdown` grants no activation, until the context runs). `audio.startDrone()` runs when the lobby's front doors close behind him.
  - **Locked doors**: walking into one makes it shudder and rattle, through the stage's bump handler. A branch may carry `bumpCount: {door, at, caption, setFlag, friction, show}`: the pushes on that locked door are counted, `show` {count: prop} reveals a prop at a given count, and on the `at`-th push the flag is set, `friction` is added and the caption lands. S3 H uses it (COUNTED_DOOR at thirty-one, handprints on the glass).
  - **Dev start**: `start(sceneId, render)` with a scene other than S0 puts him just inside that scene's room, with a score that gives the requested render, and starts the drone at once. `&conf=N` / `&diss=N` on the page URL set the score itself (conformance −8..8, dissonance 0..7): the render then comes from that score (`render` decides only while conformance is between −1 and 1), so `resolveEnding()` reads the same state (`?start=S8&conf=3`, then DELIVER, is ASSIMILATION). A score no real game could have by that scene is allowed, with a console warning. A held-entry room gets the connector he would have arrived by. The rest of the game plays on from there as normal.
  - `bail()` leads to PENDING REVIEW. `_debug()` reports the zones, links and state for the autopilot and for tests, with the driven actors, the `unseen` watches, the breath layer and the hum.
- **[[ninety-nine/src/spine.js|spine.js]]**: pure helpers over `scenes.json` and `state.js`. `outcomesFor()` gives what each threshold of a scene leads to (the next render and room, or the ending and its room), so the director can build both possible next rooms before either is chosen. Also `zonesOf()`, `baseOf()` and `cloneState()`.
- **[[ninety-nine/src/friction.js|friction.js]]**: friction from movement, now that the mini-games are gone.
  - **Hesitation** is the active seconds before committing, scaled between each scene's `t0` and `t1`. Time while carried or while the window is hidden does not count.
  - **Doubling back** counts how often he walks from one threshold's approach ring to the other's.
  - Each scene yields 0..1 (0.6 × hesitation + 0.4 × doubling back, with three alternations counting as full), summed into `state.friction` (capped at 8). Its only consumer is still `fracture()`.
- **[[ninety-nine/src/labels.js|labels.js]]**: the threshold labels. Each choice's words stand in the room over the place to walk to, projected every frame (`stage.project()`). They fade in across the approach ring and stamp when the zone is entered. A room's `labelInk` (passed by the director with each label) changes the ink: `dark` is dark words on a pale halo (S1 H's blown-out walls), `backed` light words on a translucent dark plate (the street's address). Company text: never "you" (C4).
- **[[ninety-nine/src/captions.js|captions.js]]**: spoken lines and company text that land in the room: the receptionist's "Ninety-nine." / "Shaun.", the door's violation line, the cab's refusal, the box's contents. Timed from a scene's start (`captions`) or played as `caption` beats. One line at a time: a new caption fades out whatever is still up.
- **[[ninety-nine/src/screens.js|screens.js]]**: what the building's screens say, drawn as a canvas texture onto a monitor prop's screen. Pages: the apartment's job posting, the confirmation, the countdown and ACCEPTED; the Harlowe desktop; the requisition (rows from `text/manifest.json`). `screenPower()` turns a screen on or off. A screen is part of its room, so the window cannot be closed.
- **`stage/stage.js`**: the stage. It owns the renderer, the composite pass (ACES, vignette, grain, dither, and the fade: an ending's pull-back fading out, the blackout before the card; rooms no longer join through black) and the frame loop over the world and the player. `freeze()` holds the last frame for an ending's hold: only the fade runs, so no prop, light or grain moves.
  - **Carried camera moves**: `carry(pose)` eases from wherever the camera is to a world pose; `playShot(inst, shot)` plays a named shot from `data/rooms.json`, authored in the room's own frame; `holdPose()`; `releaseShot()`.
  - **Previews** (`test/rooms.html`): `preview(key)` / `walk(key)` show a lone room; a connector walked alone starts at its sill, at the top of the flight.
  - **Turns and holds**: a carried move that turns the view more than 110° (sitting down into a chair behind him) swings the heading the short way round and eases the pitch, instead of lerping the look point through the camera. A held move ends dead still: its sway and shake stop where it arrived. Once the ending's blackout has begun, nothing moves the camera or lifts the fade.
  - **His shadow**: an unseen body that only casts a shadow stands at his feet while he walks (not while carried or swimming), so his shadow points at the core with everything else (C2).
  - **For the director**: `screenRect()` (where a prop's screen sits in the viewport, which is where the application form goes), `project()` (for the threshold labels), `onFrame()` (the director's per-frame tick), `onPreFrame()` (before the world updates its lights and atmosphere: the cab's ride moves its room there), `warm()` (compiles the shader programs of what was just placed, before he looks at it), `setBumpHandler()` (what walking into a collider means: locked doors rattle), `fadeTo()` / `blackout()` and `stop()` (no more frames once the card is up).
- **`stage/library.js`**: the open-source prop library (`assets/glb/library.glb`, from the Higgsfield scene builder's catalog). Clones a model by name and gives it the company's low-poly treatment (C1): flat shading, vertex snapping, no photo texture. A clone keeps its vertex colours (windows, tyres, rust), and the prop's `mat` slot multiplies into them; a palette texture is kept, and recoloured only with `tintMapped: true`. Every material it makes is marked `catalog`, so a `palette` prop can repaint it, and `catalogPaint()` gives a part built beside a model (an ajar car door) the same paint: the slot multiplied into the model's body paint (`paint` on the prop, from `build_rooms.py` `CAR_PAINT`; the catalog's light grey without it). Optional: box fallbacks stand in without it.
- **[[ninety-nine/src/fracture.js|fracture.js]]**: Doc 4 §5.2. Three visual-wrongness layers driven by `fracture()` (Doc 1 §2.3), capped so it reads as smooth wrongness, never a glitch. The bleed is the opposite render's palette (its room's fog, ambient and sun colours), not a still.
- **[[ninety-nine/src/audio.js|audio.js]]**: Doc 4 §7.
  - `unlock()` runs on the first activating gesture (a click to look, a key, the end of a touch; the director keeps listening until the context is running). It starts the context and the music bus, so room tone and footsteps can sound from the apartment on. It does not start the drone.
  - `startDrone()` runs when the lobby doors close behind the candidate. This is C7's single `OscillatorNode`: guarded, faded in over 2 s, never stopped or restarted until the final card.
  - `setHum(spec | null)`: the store's hum, an oscillator of its own (a saw through a low-pass, on the master bus). It copies the drone's detune and the ramp `advanceScene()` last scheduled, never writes to the drone (C7), retunes rather than restarts when set again, and fades with the beds at the card.
  - Also owns the bus (ambience / sfx / voice / music → master → limiter), per-file loudness trims, the equal-power ambience crossfade, tracked one-shots, ducking (the dive's underwater low-pass: filtered, never stopped) and the ending fade.
- **[[ninety-nine/src/music.js|music.js]]**: the liminal music bed. Generative Web Audio: an 8-bar muzak loop for the lobby, slow detuned pads after the doors close, colder voicings in the hostile render. No licensed tracks; there is an optional `{file}` slot for one. The mood per scene comes from `data/scenes.json`'s `music`.
- **[[ninety-nine/src/sfx.js|sfx.js]]**: Doc 4 §10 Phase 6. One-shot mechanical and VO SFX playback (see [[sfx-raw/Raw SFX Library]] for the raw sources these were built from), plus synthesised cues for the continuous building: `door-thud` (a door sealing behind), `door-buzz`, `deadbolt`, `elevator-motor` (as long as the ride), `splash`, `stamp`, and `breath` (a swell and fall of filtered noise, with an optional inhale: a stand-in for his breathing, never a voice). `footstep` takes a `durationMs`, so a `footfall` beat can make it heavier. A beat's `sfxOpts.volume` scales any cue (`detuneCents` is read by the requisition chime). Nothing plays before the first activating gesture or while the context is suspended, since cues scheduled on a frozen clock would all fire at once on resume.
- **[[ninety-nine/src/text.js|text.js]]**: resolves text keys against `/text`. It throws loudly on a missing key, so Doc 4 §4.1's validator has teeth at runtime.
- **[[ninety-nine/src/bail.js|bail.js]]**: Doc 4 §9. The global accessibility exit: the EXIT button and a 3-finger, 1200 ms long-press on touch. A mouse click on EXIT leaves at once. On touch, where it sits under the walking thumb, the first tap arms it (EXIT?) and only a second tap within 3 s leaves. Escape no longer bails, because under pointer lock it is the browser's "release the mouse" key. Quitting routes to PENDING REVIEW, not a hard exit.
- **[[ninety-nine/src/soundToggle.js|soundToggle.js]]**: the second persistent non-diegetic control, alongside bail.js's EXIT.
- **[[ninety-nine/src/application.js|application.js]]**: the S0 WILLING / NOT WILLING intake form (added post-launch, not part of Doc 1–4's original design). `presentForm()` lays it over the apartment's CRT while he sits at it (the director follows `stage.screenRect()`). It also gives the director the countdown's steps and pace to draw on the screen.
- **[[ninety-nine/src/dev/autopilot.js|dev/autopilot.js]]**: dev only, and loaded only with `?autopilot=SSRRSSRR`. It walks the candidate through by himself, one letter per scene S1–S8: S to succumb, R to resist.
  - Options: `&seed=H` answers the form NOT WILLING, `&bail=S4` presses EXIT on reaching that scene, `&speed=2.5` scales the walking speed. Combine it with `?start=` to walk from a later scene.
  - It steers the real controls through the same colliders, zones and doors a player meets. Routes come from a navgrid built by raycasting each room's colliders, with A* over it. A cell whose centre is inside a solid (a chair, a partition: a ray cast from inside a box misses its faces) is blocked. The grid is built again once an exit door has opened, since it was built with the door shut across it. When he is stuck, it marks the cell blocked and re-plans.
  - It logs `[autopilot]` lines to the console.
